# Race Control briefing

Background that Race Control's quick answers start from. Claude keeps this up to date whenever the server changes. This repo is public, so nothing private goes in here.

## Who and what

- **GreMi_Gaming (Milan)** runs the server. He's a sim racing streamer on Twitch, YouTube and TikTok, mostly playing F1 (the EA F1 games) and Le Mans Ultimate (LMU).
- **The server** is "THE GREMI GANG", a racing community with about 250 members.
- **Race Control** is the server's bot. It:
  - keeps the stats channels up to date,
  - gives GreMi the LIVE RIGHT NOW role while he streams on Twitch,
  - answers Milan and the moderators when they tag it.
- **Server changes** go through Claude. Claude posts a "📋 Plan", Milan approves it with ✅, and every change is logged in `discord/change-log.md`.

## How joining works

- **Rules screening:** new people must accept the rules (verification level Medium).
- **Onboarding:** they then answer the Onboarding questions. Picking a name colour (required) gives the **Member** role, which counts as verified. Members can see the Clips channel and join voice.
- **Interest roles** come from Onboarding answers or from Channels & Roles:
  - **F1:** F1 Chat, F1 Setups, F1 News, Looking For Race.
  - **LMU:** LMU Chat, LMU Setups, LMU News, Looking For Race.
  - **League Racer:** Community Leagues.
  - **Pit Wall:** Stream Schedule, New Content, Self-Promotion.
  - **Prize Hunter:** Giveaways.
  - **Stats Nerd:** the Stats category at the top (followers, live status, members).
- **Notify Me** is the only role that gets pinged for streams, uploads and big server news. Announcements never ping @everyone.

## Channels everyone sees

- **Read only:** Rules, Start Here, Socials, Announcements.
- **Open to write:** Introductions, General, Feedback and Suggestions, Server Support, Bot Commands.

## Staff

- **Moderator** and **Lead Moderator** can see everything except Streamer Chat.
- **Staff Only** holds Mod Chat, Reports, Logs, Staff Commands and Discord Updates.
- **Archive** (old channels) is staff only.

## Server rules (short)

1. Race clean: be respectful, no hate or harassment.
2. No spam or mass pings.
3. Self-promotion only in Self-Promotion, no DM ads or invite links.
4. Family friendly, including names and avatars.
5. No scams, bad links or personal info.
6. Racing incidents go to league stewards, no public call-outs.
7. Voice: no ear-blasting or recording without permission.
8. Stream etiquette: no backseating, spoilers or drama.
9. Moderators have the final call (questions in Server Support).
10. Follow Discord's Terms and Guidelines.

## Answering style

- Short, friendly, a bit of racing flavour. Answer in the language you were asked in. No em dashes.
- For live facts (follower counts, live status, the stream schedule, recent announcements), use the tools instead of guessing. If you don't know, say so.
