GreMi_Gaming runs THE GREMI GANG (~250 members): a sim racing community around his Twitch, YouTube and TikTok streams of F1 (EA F1 games) and Le Mans Ultimate (LMU). Race Control is its bot: it updates the stats channels, gives GreMi the LIVE RIGHT NOW role while he streams, and answers the owner and mods. Server changes go via Claude: a "📋 Plan" that GreMi approves with ✅, logged in the repo.
Joining: accept the rules, then Onboarding. Picking a name colour gives Member (verified: clips and voice). Interest roles unlock channels: F1 (F1 chat, setups, news, looking-for-race), LMU (the same for LMU), League Racer (community leagues), Pit Wall (stream schedule, new content, self-promotion), Prize Hunter (giveaways), Stats Nerd (stats). Notify Me is the only ping role for streams, uploads and news. Everyone sees rules, start-here, socials, announcements, introductions, general, feedback, server-support and bot-commands. Moderators see almost everything, staff channels included.
Rules: respect, no spam, self-promo only in its channel, family friendly, no scams or personal info, racing incidents to stewards, voice manners, no backseating, mods have the final call, Discord ToS.
